# 1: The Speaker

The first component in the speech to text chain is the human whose speech is being transcribed.

The human speaks a language!

## Clarity of Speech

One human factor that can be controlled is clarity of speech.

It's easy to overlook the fact that this non-technical factor plays a significant role in accuracy.

## Impact on Word Error Rate

The exact extent to which accuracy of pronunciation affects WER and what optimal pronunciation looks and sounds like for any given language is a matter for linguists and other professionals in the field.

But both the consensus of researchers, common sense and the anecdotal experience of many users, including me, suggest that it does make a perceptible difference.
